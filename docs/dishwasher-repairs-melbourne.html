Common Faults

Dishwasher Problems We Diagnose and Repair

Dishwasher Not Draining

Water left in the bottom of a dishwasher may be caused by a blocked filter, restricted drain hose, obstructed sump, faulty drain pump, or another drainage problem.

We inspect the drainage system to determine whether the problem can be resolved by clearing a blockage or whether a component requires repair or replacement. Avoid running another cycle if the dishwasher remains full of water or begins leaking.

Dishes Not Coming Out Clean

Poor cleaning results may be connected to blocked spray arms, dirty filters, low water pressure, a faulty wash pump, a failed diverter, or a detergent dispenser problem.

We assess the dishwasher's water supply, circulation, and spray performance to identify the cause. We can also advise whether loading practices, detergent selection, or routine maintenance may be affecting the result.

Dishwasher Not Drying

If dishes remain wet after the cycle, the dishwasher may have a failed heating element, faulty temperature sensor, rinse aid problem, or issue with the drying system.

Some modern dishwashers use condensation drying rather than exposed heating elements, so results can vary between models. We test the relevant components and explain whether the problem is mechanical or related to the selected program.

Dishwasher Leaking Water

Dishwasher leaks may come from a worn door seal, damaged hose, loose connection, cracked component, failed pump, excessive foaming, or overfill fault.

Stop using the dishwasher if water is escaping onto the floor or into surrounding cabinetry. Continued operation could cause further water damage or activate the appliance's flood protection system.

Dishwasher Not Filling With Water

A dishwasher that will not fill may have a restricted water supply, blocked inlet screen, faulty inlet valve, float switch problem, door latch fault, or activated flood protection system.

We test the water supply and relevant components to identify what is preventing the appliance from filling. If an error code appears, include the full code when booking your repair.

Dishwasher Not Starting or Displaying an Error Code

Starting problems may involve the door latch, control board, touch panel, power connection, sensor, or flood protection system. An error code can help identify which part of the dishwasher has detected a problem.

Record the complete code or take a clear photograph of the display before turning off the appliance. We will use this information as part of the diagnosis rather than relying on the code alone.

Repair or Replace?

Is Your Dishwasher Worth Repairing?

Many dishwasher faults can be addressed by repairing or replacing an individual component rather than replacing the entire appliance. Pumps, inlet valves, door seals, latches, heating elements, sensors, hoses, and spray components are among the parts that may cause an otherwise reliable dishwasher to stop working correctly.

The right decision depends on the dishwasher's age, condition, original quality, fault, required parts, and repair cost. Integrated and premium dishwashers may be particularly worthwhile repairing because replacement can also involve matching cabinetry panels and completing additional installation work.

After diagnosing the fault, we explain what has failed and provide an honest recommendation. You can then compare the fixed repair price with the dishwasher's condition and replacement value before deciding how to proceed.

Why is there water sitting in the bottom of my dishwasher?

A small amount of water around the filter may be normal for some dishwasher models. A larger pool of water usually indicates a blocked filter, obstructed drain hose, restricted sump, faulty drain pump, or another drainage problem.

Check and clean the accessible filter according to the manufacturer's instructions. If the water remains or quickly returns, stop running additional cycles and arrange a professional assessment.

Why are my dishes still dirty after a complete wash cycle?

Dishes may remain dirty because of blocked spray arms, clogged filters, insufficient water, a failing wash pump, a faulty diverter, incorrect loading, or unsuitable detergent. A heavily overloaded dishwasher can also prevent water from reaching every item.

Clean the accessible filter and check that the spray arms can rotate freely. If cleaning performance remains poor, the dishwasher may require diagnostic testing.

Why is my dishwasher not drying properly?

Poor drying may be caused by a faulty heating element, temperature sensor problem, empty rinse aid dispenser, incorrect program, or problem with the appliance's drying system. Plastic items may also retain more moisture than glass or ceramic dishes.

Different dishwasher models use different drying methods. We can assess the appliance and determine whether its performance is normal or a component requires attention.

Is it safe to use a dishwasher that is leaking?

Stop using the dishwasher if water is leaking onto the floor, beneath the appliance, or into surrounding cabinetry. Continuing to run it may worsen the leak, damage kitchen materials, or allow water to reach electrical components.

Turn the dishwasher off and close its water supply if it is safe to do so. Arrange a professional assessment before using the appliance again.
