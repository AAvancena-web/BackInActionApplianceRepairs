Common Faults

Washing Machine Problems We Diagnose and Repair

Washing Machine Not Draining

A washing machine that will not drain may have a blocked filter, restricted hose, faulty drain pump, or another problem affecting the drainage system.

We inspect the relevant components to determine why water is remaining inside the drum. Once the fault has been identified, we explain the recommended repair and provide a fixed-price quote.

Washing Machine Not Spinning

Spin problems may be connected to an unbalanced load, worn drive component, motor fault, speed sensor issue, door lock problem, or another internal failure.

We test the machine to identify the cause rather than relying only on the visible symptom. This gives you a clearer understanding of what needs attention.

Washing Machine Leaking Water

Water may escape from the door seal, inlet hose, drain hose, pump, detergent dispenser, tub, or an internal connection.

Turn the washing machine off and stop using it if you notice a significant leak. Our technician can examine the appliance, locate the source, and recommend an appropriate solution.

Washing Machine Making Loud Noises

Banging, grinding, scraping, or squealing noises may indicate problems with the drum bearings, suspension system, shock absorbers, pump, motor, or an object trapped inside the machine.

The type of noise and the point in the cycle when it occurs can provide useful diagnostic information. Tell us what you hear when booking your appointment.

Washing Machine Door Will Not Open or Lock

A washing machine door may remain locked when water has not drained correctly or when the door interlock, handle, sensor, or control system has developed a fault.

Avoid forcing the door open, as this may damage the lock, handle, or surrounding components. We can diagnose the cause and explain the safest repair option.

Washing Machine Not Filling or Starting

If the washing machine will not fill or start, there may be an issue with the water supply, inlet valve, door lock, power connection, control system, or another electrical component.

We inspect and test the relevant systems to identify the underlying problem. If an error code is displayed, include it with your booking details.

Repair or Replace?

Is Your Washing Machine Worth Repairing?

The right decision depends on the washing machine's age, overall condition, original quality, fault, required parts, and likely repair cost. A single failed component does not always mean the entire appliance needs to be replaced.

Problems involving pumps, belts, door locks, inlet valves, hoses, and seals may often be resolved without replacing the machine. More extensive problems involving bearings, tubs, motors, or electronic control systems may require a closer comparison between repair costs and replacement value.

After diagnosing the fault, we will explain what is required and provide an honest opinion. You will receive the information needed to decide whether repairing your washing machine is the practical choice for your household.

Do you repair front-loading and top-loading washing machines?

Tell us which type of washing machine you have when booking. Providing the brand and model number can also help us prepare for the appointment.

What washing machine problems can you repair?

We diagnose and repair washing machines that will not drain, spin, fill, start, lock, unlock, or complete a cycle. We can also investigate leaks, loud noises, excessive movement, error codes, and poor washing performance.

The same symptom can have several possible causes. We test the machine to identify the underlying fault before recommending a repair.

Can you repair a washing machine displaying an error code?

Yes. An error code can provide useful information about the system detecting a problem, but it does not always identify the exact part that has failed.

Record the complete code as it appears and include it when booking. A photograph of the display may also help us understand the reported fault before the appointment.

Should I continue using my washing machine if it is leaking?

Stop using the washing machine if you notice a significant or continuing leak. Turn the appliance off and close the water supply taps if it is safe to do so.

Continuing to operate a leaking machine may cause floor damage or allow water to reach electrical components. Arrange an assessment so the source of the leak can be identified.

Why is my washing machine not draining or spinning?

A washing machine may stop draining because of a blocked filter, restricted hose, faulty pump, or control problem. A machine that will not spin may have an unbalanced load, door lock issue, worn drive component, motor fault, or sensor problem.

These symptoms may also be connected. If the machine cannot drain the water, it may not begin the spin cycle. A diagnostic assessment is the most reliable way to determine the cause.

Can you complete the washing machine repair during the first visit?

We aim to complete repairs during the first visit whenever the fault, accessibility, and parts availability allow. Our technician carries commonly required washing machine parts in the van.

Some repairs may require a model-specific component that needs to be ordered. If a return visit is necessary, we will explain the next step and arrange a suitable time with you.

How much does a washing machine repair cost?

The cost depends on the diagnosed fault, the replacement parts required, the washing machine model, and the work involved. An accurate repair price cannot always be provided before the appliance has been assessed.

After diagnosing the problem, we provide a fixed-price quote before approved repair work begins. You can then decide whether you want to proceed.

Is it better to repair or replace my washing machine?

Repair may be worthwhile when the washing machine is otherwise in good condition and the fault involves a replaceable component. Replacement may be more practical when the appliance has several major problems or the repair cost is high compared with its age and value.

We assess the fault and explain the available options without pressuring you into a repair. The final decision remains with you.

What information should I provide when choosing a washing machine repair service?

Provide the washing machine type, brand, model number, approximate age, and a detailed description of the problem. Mention error codes, leaks, unusual noises, smells, excessive movement, and the point in the cycle when the fault occurs.

You should also provide your suburb and explain when the problem began. Accurate information helps us understand the enquiry, prepare for the appointment, and confirm whether we can assist with your machine.
