Which appliances do you repair?

We specialise exclusively in washing machines, clothes dryers, and dishwashers. Our focused service means we do not divide our attention between every type of household appliance.

By concentrating on these three categories, we can develop detailed knowledge of common faults and carry parts that are frequently required. This helps us diagnose problems efficiently and complete many repairs during the first visit.

Which areas do you cover for appliance repairs in Melbourne?

We service households across the Melbourne metropolitan area, including many inner, northern, western, eastern, south-eastern, and bayside suburbs.

Service availability depends on your location and our current appointment schedule. Contact us with your suburb, and we will confirm whether it falls within our service area and provide the next available booking window.

How much does an appliance repair cost?

Repair costs vary depending on the appliance, the fault, the parts required, and the work involved. We first diagnose the problem so that we can provide an accurate repair recommendation.

You will receive a fixed-price quote before any repair work begins. There is no obligation to proceed if you decide that the quoted repair is not the right option for you.

Can you repair my appliance on the same day?

Same-day and next-day appointments are available where possible. Availability depends on your suburb, the time of your enquiry, and the existing booking schedule.

Contacting us earlier in the day may improve the likelihood of securing a same-day appointment. We will always provide realistic availability rather than promising a time that we cannot meet.

Which appliance brands do you repair?

We repair many major washing machine, dryer, and dishwasher brands sold in Australia. These include Bosch, Miele, LG, Samsung, Fisher and Paykel, Electrolux, Westinghouse, Simpson, Haier, Asko, Smeg, Whirlpool, and several others.

Provide the brand and model number when booking if this information is available. It can help us understand the appliance configuration and prepare suitable parts before the visit.

Do you guarantee your appliance repairs?

Yes. Every completed repair is supported by our workmanship guarantee. We stand behind the quality of the work we perform and want you to feel confident when choosing Back In Action.

The applicable coverage will be explained as part of the repair process. If you experience a concern related to the completed work, contact us with your repair details so we can assess the situation.

Can you tell me whether I should repair or replace my appliance?

Yes. After diagnosing the fault, we will explain whether repair appears to be a practical option based on the appliance's condition, age, and repair requirements.

Some faults can be resolved economically and extend the useful life of an otherwise reliable appliance. In other cases, replacement may offer better long-term value. We will give you straightforward advice and allow you to make the final decision.

What should I do if my appliance is leaking, overheating, or producing a burning smell?

Stop using the appliance if it is leaking heavily, overheating, producing smoke, creating a strong burning smell, or causing electrical problems. Switch it off if it is safe to do so and keep the surrounding area clear.

Contact us and describe exactly what happened, including when the issue occurred and whether the appliance displayed an error code. Do not continue testing an appliance if doing so could create a safety risk or worsen the fault.

What happens during an appliance repair appointment?

Your technician will inspect the appliance, discuss the symptoms you have noticed, and carry out appropriate diagnostic tests. Once the fault has been identified, you will receive an explanation and a fixed-price quote.

The repair will only begin after you approve the price. Where possible, we will complete the work during the same visit, test the appliance, and confirm that it is operating correctly before leaving.

What information should I provide when booking an appliance repair?

Tell us whether the appliance is a washing machine, dryer, or dishwasher. Include the brand, model number, approximate age, suburb, and a clear description of what the appliance is doing.

Mention any error codes, leaks, unusual noises, smells, or changes in performance. Providing complete information helps us understand the likely problem, prepare for the appointment, and confirm whether we can assist with your appliance.
