Which appliances do you repair?

We specialise in washing machines, clothes dryers and dishwashers. Focusing on these three means we carry the right parts and get most jobs done on the first visit.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover the Melbourne metro area. Call us with your suburb and we will confirm your booking window.

How much does a repair cost?

Every appliance and fault is different, so we diagnose first and then give you a fixed price before any work starts. You only go ahead if you are happy with the quote.

We offer same day and next day appointments where available. Call early in the day for the best chance of a same day visit.

We repair all the major brands sold in Australia, including Bosch, Miele, LG, Samsung, Fisher & Paykel, Electrolux, Westinghouse, Simpson and Haier.

Yes. Every repair is covered by our workmanship guarantee. If the same fault comes back, so do we.
