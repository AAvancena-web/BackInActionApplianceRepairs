Which appliances do you repair?

We specialise exclusively in washing machines, clothes dryers, and dishwashers. We repair common faults affecting the operation, performance, drainage, heating, controls, and other essential functions of these appliances.

Our focused service allows us to build detailed knowledge of the appliances we repair regularly. We do not currently repair refrigerators, freezers, ovens, cooktops, or other household appliances.

Which Melbourne areas do you service?

We provide in-home appliance repairs across selected suburbs within metropolitan Melbourne. Our service area includes many inner, northern, western, eastern, south-eastern, and bayside locations.

Coverage and appointment availability may vary depending on your suburb. Provide your location when calling or completing the booking form, and we will confirm whether we can come to your address.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Tell us whether the appliance is a washing machine, clothes dryer, or dishwasher. Include the brand, model number, approximate age, suburb, and a clear description of what has gone wrong.

Mention any leaks, error codes, unusual noises, burning smells, drainage problems, or changes in performance. The more relevant information you provide, the better prepared we can be for the appointment.

How much will my appliance repair cost?

Repair costs depend on the appliance, diagnosed fault, replacement parts, and work required. An accurate repair price cannot always be provided before the appliance has been assessed.

After identifying the fault, we will explain what needs attention and provide a fixed-price quote before approved work begins. You can review the recommendation and decide whether you want to proceed.

Can I arrange a same-day appliance repair?

Same-day and next-day appointments may be available depending on your suburb, the time of your enquiry, and our existing booking schedule. Calling earlier in the day may improve your chances of securing an earlier appointment.

We will provide realistic availability when you contact us. If a same-day booking is unavailable, we will offer the next suitable appointment within your service area.

Do you repair appliances in my home?

Yes. We diagnose and repair washing machines, dryers, and dishwashers at your home. This saves you from moving or transporting a large and heavy appliance to a workshop.

We carry commonly required parts to help complete more repairs during the first appointment. Some model-specific components may need to be ordered before the repair can be completed.

Which appliance brands do you repair?

We work with many major washing machine, dryer, and dishwasher brands sold in Australia. Service availability can depend on the appliance model, age, fault, and access to suitable replacement parts.

Provide the brand and model number when making your enquiry. This information helps us identify the appliance configuration and determine whether we can assist.

What should I do if my appliance is leaking or producing a burning smell?

Stop using the appliance if it is leaking heavily, overheating, producing smoke, or creating a strong burning smell. Switch it off and disconnect it from the power supply if it is safe to do so.

Do not repeatedly restart the appliance to test whether the problem has disappeared. Contact us with a clear description of what happened so we can discuss the appropriate next step.

What happens after I submit the booking form?

After receiving your enquiry, we will contact you to discuss the appliance, the symptoms you have noticed, and your location. We may ask for additional details, such as the model number, error code, or installation type.

We will then confirm service coverage and discuss a suitable appointment. Submitting the form does not automatically confirm a booking until we have contacted you and agreed on an appointment time.

What information should I provide when choosing an appliance repair service?

Provide the appliance type, brand, model number, approximate age, and a detailed description of the problem. You should also include your suburb and explain when the fault first appeared.

Ask about the diagnostic process, pricing, workmanship coverage, and whether the technician regularly repairs your type of appliance. Clear information helps you compare services and choose an appliance repair provider with confidence.
